## 1. Product Overview

### Product Name

**Make My Marriage**

- Groom

- Groom's parents

# 2. Product Vision

Indian weddings involve significantly more coordination than a single wedding-day event.

A wedding may include:

- Engagement
- Haldi
- Mehendi
- Sangeet
- Wedding ceremony
- Reception
- Pooja
- Family gatherings
- Regional ceremonies
- Custom events

Each of these events may involve different:

- Guests
- Venues
- Family members
- Vendors
- Tasks
- Timelines

Currently, families usually coordinate all of this using a combination of:

- WhatsApp groups
- Phone calls
- Excel sheets
- Notes
- Diaries
- Printed guest lists
- Multiple family members maintaining separate information

This creates confusion and duplication.

**Make My Marriage aims to become the single source of truth for the family throughout the wedding planning journey.**

---

# 3. Problem Statement

Planning an Indian wedding often involves multiple family members handling different responsibilities simultaneously.

This creates several problems.

### Information is scattered

Wedding information exists across:

- WhatsApp
- Phone contacts
- Spreadsheets
- Notes
- Images
- Documents
- Family members' personal devices

There is no centralized system.

### Responsibilities are unclear

Family members frequently discuss responsibilities verbally.

For example:

- Who will contact the photographer?
- Who is confirming the venue?
- Who will call a particular guest?
- Who is handling decoration?
- Who is following up with the caterer?

These responsibilities may be forgotten or duplicated.

### Guest management becomes difficult

Indian weddings often involve hundreds of guests.

Different guests may also be invited to different events.

For example:

A family may be invited to:

- Wedding
- Reception

but not necessarily:

- Haldi
- Mehendi
- Sangeet

Managing this manually becomes difficult.

### RSVP tracking is inefficient

Families often call guests individually or manually track attendance through WhatsApp.

This makes it difficult to know:

- Who has confirmed
- Who declined
- Who has not responded
- Which event the guest is attending

### Vendor information is distributed

Different relatives may communicate with different vendors.

Important details such as:

- Contact information
- Services
- Confirmation status
- Event assignment
- Follow-up dates
- Documents

can easily become scattered.

### Wedding-day coordination becomes stressful

Wedding events involve many time-sensitive activities.

Without a shared timeline, family members may not know:

- What needs to happen
- At what time
- Where
- Who is responsible

---

# 4. Product Objective

The primary objective of Make My Marriage is:

> To help Indian families collaboratively organize and manage their entire wedding from one centralized digital workspace.

The product should reduce dependency on:

- Multiple spreadsheets
- Repeated phone calls
- Separate guest lists
- Unorganized WhatsApp conversations
- Personal notes

---

# 5. Product Goals

The first version of Make My Marriage should allow families to:

1. Create their wedding workspace.

2. Add the bride and groom information.

3. Create multiple wedding events.

4. Add and manage family members.

5. Create wedding-related tasks.

6. Assign tasks to family members.

7. Manage guests.

8. Organize guests into families or groups.

9. Invite different guests to different events.

10. Send digital invitation links.

11. Collect RSVP responses.

12. Track guest attendance.

13. Add wedding vendors.

14. Associate vendors with events.

15. Track vendor follow-ups.

16. Create event schedules.

17. Create a wedding website.

18. Maintain a wedding photo gallery.

19. View the overall wedding status from a central dashboard.

# 6. Product Principles

The product should follow the following principles.

## 6.1 Family First

The application should be designed around family collaboration rather than professional wedding planners.

---

## 6.2 Simple Over Complex

Wedding planning itself is already complicated.

The application should make planning simpler rather than introducing additional complexity.

---

## 6.3 Event-Centric Planning

A wedding should not be treated as one single event.

The product should support multiple ceremonies and functions.

---

## 6.4 One Source of Truth

All important wedding information should be accessible from one wedding workspace.

---

## 6.5 Mobile-Friendly Experience

Many family members will access the application primarily from their phones.

The experience should therefore remain easy to use across smaller screens.

---

## 6.6 Guest Simplicity

Guests should not need to create an account simply to:

- View an invitation
- View event information
- RSVP

# 70. Important Business Rules

### BR-001

A wedding must have at least one bride and groom record.

### BR-002

A guest does not automatically belong to every event.

### BR-003

Guests must explicitly be associated with invited events.

### BR-004

RSVP must be tracked at the event level.

### BR-005

Vendors are internally managed records and are not application users.

### BR-006

Family members have common access in the initial release.

### BR-007

Guests do not require accounts for invitation and RSVP.

### BR-008

A task may exist independently or be associated with an event.

### BR-009

A vendor may be associated with multiple events.

### BR-010

A wedding may contain multiple events.

- Tablets

Important information should remain understandable through:

- Clear labels
- Readable text
- Appropriate contrast
- Keyboard-friendly interactions where possible
- Meaningful form validation

---

## Reliability

Users should not unexpectedly lose information while creating or editing important wedding data.

---

## Privacy

Private wedding information should not automatically become publicly accessible.

Private family information, guest information and wedding information should be accessible only to authorized users or through intentionally shared guest-facing links.

---

### Event Cancelled

---

### Guest Changes RSVP

---

### Guest Attends Only Selected Events

Attendance may differ by ceremony.

---

### Guest Group Size Changes

A family may originally expect four attendees but later confirm only two.

---

### Vendor Cancelled

A confirmed vendor may later become unavailable.

---

A family member may no longer participate.

Existing tasks and activity history should remain understandable.

---

### Event Venue Changes

Venue information may change after invitations have already been shared.

Updated information should be visible wherever relevant.

# 83. Product Statement

**Make My Marriage helps Indian families plan their wedding together from one place.**

Instead of managing events, tasks, guests, vendors and wedding information across separate WhatsApp groups, spreadsheets and notebooks, the family receives one shared wedding workspace.

The first version will remain deliberately focused on:

**Wedding → Events → Tasks → Guests/RSVP → Vendor Management → Family Collaboration → Wedding Website/Gallery**

Everything outside these areas should be considered only after the core product has been validated with real users.
